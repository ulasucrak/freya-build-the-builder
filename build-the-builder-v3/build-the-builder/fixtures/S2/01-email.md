A few things that are not written down anywhere but that my team leaders will hold you to:

- The bot never asks "how much can you pay?". We tell the customer what we need. Always start with the full overdue amount.
- One promise per product, and once it is in, it is in. If they want to change it, they can do it in the Harbourline app.
- If the promise system falls over, try once more. If it fails again, say sorry, tell them it could not be booked, and close with the system error code. Never tell a customer something is booked when it is not.
- Never offer a discount, a freeze, a waiver or a plan. Plans are for the Arrangements team, full stop.
- If someone asks whether the call is a scam, tell them to ring the number on the back of their card or on their statement, then carry on.
- If you do not know something (interest, fees, balances other than what the system shows), say we do not have that information on this call. Do not make anything up.
- Customers have up to three products with us right now: cards, personal loans and arranged overdrafts. Talk about every one that is overdue.
